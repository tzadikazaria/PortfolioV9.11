Marketplace
product visuals.

Designing scalable product visuals for beauty and wellness brands across Asian e-commerce marketplaces, turning product benefits into clear, consistent listing systems.

Role
Digital designer
Focus
Scalable listings
Markets
Asia / E-commerce
Output
Product visual systems

01Challenge

Clear fast. Consistent everywhere.

This work was part of OG Global Import’s international e-commerce operations across Asian markets, where beauty and wellness brands were sold through several marketplaces. It is separate from Fine Rituals, OG Global Import’s Israeli online store. Each marketplace needed product visuals that explained the benefits quickly and clearly, often in limited space and with different image requirements.

The challenge was a scalable visual system that adapts to different brands and product types while staying consistent, informative and on-brand. My work covers the listing visuals, not the brands’ own identities.

02What I designed

Four parts of one system.

I designed the icons and the listing images, and prepared them for each platform.

  1. 01Icon libraryProduct-benefit icons, each communicating a single claim in one visual language.
  2. 02Scalable systemThe same icon style applied across product categories for coherence.
  3. 03Photography integrationFull listing images that combine icons with product photography.
  4. 04Platform deliverySized and formatted to each platform’s requirements across several Asian markets.

03Benefit language

One icon. One message.

Each icon is a thin line drawing in a circle and carries one claim, from hydrating and long-lasting to vegan and cruelty-free. The shared frame keeps them consistent across categories, and keeps listings easy to scan.

04Product families

Same logic. New product.

Icons, modular layouts and product-led images communicate key benefits quickly. The same structure adapts to each product, while photography, colour and tone follow it.

05Listing structure

From hero to detail.

A listing set opens with the product, explains what makes it different, then shows it in use.

06Platforms & markets

One system. Several platforms.

The visual language stays the same while sizes, hierarchy and the amount of information change to suit each platform’s requirements. Keeping the layouts modular makes them easier to localise and to reuse for new products.

07Delivered

What the work covered.

  • Icon libraryBenefit icons in one style.
  • Listing image setsPer product, combining icons and photography.
  • Platform versionsSized for each marketplace.
