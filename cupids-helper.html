Cupid’s Helper

An app concept for planning a date: curated venue ideas, sharing the plan with a partner, and a soft romantic look.

01Challenge

Planning a date is hard work.

Planning a perfect date is stressful and time-consuming. People often struggle to find activities both partners will enjoy. Cupid’s Helper offers a curated selection of date ideas shaped by the user’s preferences, so romantic planning feels simple and enjoyable.

02Planning journey

One date, four steps.

In the scenario, Ronan wants a romantic date and opens the app to plan it. He signs in, searches, and gets several ideas from the app’s database: movies, restaurants, concerts and art galleries. He reads the details of each option, picks one, and shares it with Rona so they can coordinate and meet on time.

  1. 01Sign inOpen the app and log in.
  2. 02BrowseMovies, restaurants, concerts and galleries.
  3. 03CompareRead each option’s details.
  4. 04ShareSend the chosen date to a partner.

03Key screens

Built around browsing.

The home screen puts category icons at the top and recommended venues below as image cards. A venue screen shows a large photo, rating and details. The interface is in Hebrew, set right to left.

04Visual identity

Soft pinks and purples.

The visual design aims for an inviting, romantic look: a palette of soft pinks and purples, with considered typography and icons, to make the stressful job of planning a date feel easy and pleasant.
