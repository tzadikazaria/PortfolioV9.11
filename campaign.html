01Context

Different brands, across digital channels.

Fine Rituals was OG Global Import’s Israeli online retail store, a boutique beauty and lifestyle retailer carrying several brands, each with its own products, audiences and visual identity. I designed digital campaigns, social media assets, newsletters and website visuals for this retail activity, promoting those products for launches, sales and brand-awareness moments.

Each piece had to work on the website, social, paid media and email without losing its core visual idea. The brand identities of the featured products are not my work; the layouts, adaptations and campaign assets are.

02My role

Design and adaptation.

I worked on the visual design and production of campaign creative, adapting each idea for e-commerce, social, paid media and email. I worked alongside marketing, sales and operations, and prepared assets for Meta Ads, Google Ads and Flashy.

03Campaign system

A flexible visual language.

Offers, launches and awareness posts share a simple structure: one product or portrait as the focus, a short message, and a clear offer. The layout flexes to fit each product and brand.

04Formats

The same idea, different formats.

Product-in-use shots, hero visuals and bundle offers were adapted to different formats, including a Yom Ha’atzmaut post and a web and commerce piece. Holiday promotions were laid out as both square and vertical versions of the same design.

05Selected campaign

Flame Sale.

The Flame Sale was laid out as a website banner and a social post (and a newsletter, shown below). The same dark red look carries across all three.

06Selected campaign

The White Holiday.

A three-story series, each story with its own offer: up to 25% on selected collections, 20% on home products, and 15% on skincare and fragrance. A product group, a candle close-up and a portrait share one layout and one type treatment.

07Web & social

Social, web and paid.

Campaign creative sat inside the online store as homepage banners, promotional graphics and product pages, and in the social feed as posts and stories.

08Story ads

Shown in the feed.

Story creative as it appears in Instagram, each with a Shop now button.

09Email & newsletter design

The campaign, in the inbox.

Newsletters carry the same campaign language into a direct channel for launches and promotions. The layouts are single-column: a header, a hero visual, then offer or product blocks with clear shop buttons. Some add customer reviews and an Instagram strip.

Both newsletters shown use Hebrew copy set right to left.

10Campaign operations

Beyond the layout.

I prepared campaign assets for Meta Ads, Google Ads and Flashy, and took part in newsletter scheduling and automation setup.
