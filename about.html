I’m Tzadik Azaria, a digital and visual designer with in-house and freelance experience. I create e-commerce content, digital campaigns, social media assets, web visuals and print materials, with an eye for typography, layout and brand consistency.

Background

I’ve worked in-house on e-commerce and campaign content for beauty and wellness brands, and as a freelancer on branding, signage, brochures and presentations for clients in different industries. I’m used to taking a project from the brief and concept through to final production, and I enjoy adapting one visual idea to different channels and markets while keeping it consistent.

I studied media design at ORT Hermelin College, and I’m looking for a full-time role in a design studio, agency or in-house creative team.
